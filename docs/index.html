Privacy policy for ADB Local Shell PRO.

Effective date: 8 November 2026
Developer: PT Deploy Dulu Pulang Nanti
Package: com.deploydulupulangnanti.adblocalshellpro

The app does not automatically collect or send your personal data to us. There are no ads, analytics or third-party crash reporting SDKs, and no account is required.

1. What the app processes locally

The app connects to Android's ADB service only at 127.0.0.1 on the same device. It processes commands you enter and the output Android returns. Depending on your commands, this can include device information, package names, logs or other sensitive information. Commands can themselves instruct Android to access files, change settings or communicate over a network. Those actions depend on the command you choose to run.

2. Local storage and retention

  • Pairing identity: an ADB private key generated for this installation, kept in app-private storage excluded from Android backup. Pairing codes are used during pairing and are not written to app storage.
  • Preferences: the last connection port, terminal text size and history preference, stored locally.
  • Saved commands: custom command names and text, kept until you delete them.
  • Optional history: up to 100 commands, timestamps, exit codes, execution times and up to 16 KB of output per entry. New entries replace older ones. History is enabled by default; you can disable future saves and clear existing entries. Disabling history does not delete existing history.
  • Live output: terminal output stays in memory until cleared or the app process ends.

3. Permissions and device access

Internet permission permits the local ADB socket connection. Network-state access supports network compatibility. The app requests no account, location, camera, microphone, contacts, advertising identifier or broad storage permission. Android's Wireless debugging authorization provides shell-level access for commands you choose; it does not grant root access.

4. Copying and exporting

You can copy output to the Android clipboard or export text through Android's system document picker. Android and your selected destination apps or providers handle those copies, and export destinations can include cloud storage. Review output before copying, exporting or sending it. Those copies remain outside this app until you delete them there.

5. Deletion and revocation

Clear history in the History tab and delete custom commands in the Commands tab. Clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to remove its local data and ADB private key. To revoke Android's authorization, also remove ADB Local Shell PRO from Paired devices in Wireless debugging settings. Turn Wireless debugging off when not needed. Uninstalling does not remove exported files or clipboard copies.

6. Support communications and purchases

If you email us, your provider delivers your email address and any message or attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and retain them only as needed to handle your request and applicable obligations. Please do not send pairing secrets or sensitive logs. Google Play handles paid app purchases and refunds under Google's policies. The app does not receive or store payment card details.

7. Children

This developer tool is intended for people who understand Android debugging and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal data. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal data by email.

8. Security and policy changes

App-private storage and paired Wireless debugging help protect local data, but no device or storage method guarantees absolute security. Rooted devices, compromised operating systems and exposed exports can present additional risks. If our data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date.
